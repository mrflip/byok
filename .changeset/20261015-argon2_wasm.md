New package, `safebyok-fastkey`: Argon2id through WebAssembly (`hash-wasm`) as a safebyok KeyDeriver, about four times faster than the core's `@noble/hashes` deriver and deriving the same bytes. `argon2idWasmWebV1Deriver` stands in for `argon2idWebV1Deriver` under the same `argon2id_web_v1` label, so a vault made under either opens under the other; `argon2idWasmDeriver(spec)` runs a spec of the host's own. It needs WebAssembly (not React Native's Hermes) and, in a page with a Content-Security-Policy, `'wasm-unsafe-eval'`.

The core exports `parseKeyDeriverSpec(spec)`, the check `argon2idDeriver` makes of a spec, for a deriver over another Argon2id implementation to make the same. Its refusal now begins "Argon2id cannot run that keyDeriverSpec" where it named `argon2idDeriver`. Nothing stored changes.
