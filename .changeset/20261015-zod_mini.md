Smaller bundles: safebyok now validates with `zod/mini`, and its build keeps each module its own file, so a bundler keeps only what your imports reach. Everything together falls from about 36 kB to 21 kB (minified and brotlied, zod included), `openVault` from 29 kB to 14 kB, and `stableJsonify` alone from 25 kB to 2.3 kB. The zod peer range is unchanged (`^4.0.0`).

**Breaking, for code that builds on the exported schemas** (`Label`, `VaultScope`, `Lockbox`, `LockboxSummary`, `VaultBundle`, `Ciphered`, `Whisper`): they are `zod/mini` schemas now. `.parse` and `.safeParse` work as before, and they still fit inside a classic Zod schema of your own, but classic's chained methods are gone from them: write `z.optional(Label)` for `Label.optional()`, and `z.omit(Lockbox, { ... })` (from `zod/mini`) or `z.object(Lockbox.shape).omit({ ... })` for `Lockbox.omit({ ... })`.
