New entry `safebyok-convex/testing`: `fakeDeployment(host)`, a Convex deployment in memory that the real `ConvexClient`, or any other Convex client you connect with `deployment.connectWith(subject, make)` (a `ConvexReactClient`, say), talks to over a fake WebSocket. `host` is the app you built with `convex-test`'s `defineTestApp(...).defineModules(...)`; the entry imports `convex` and nothing else. For the tests of an adapter, a binding or an app, never for production: it runs one call at a time and cannot prove the network or a real server. Nothing already exported changes; the package's dev dependency on `react` goes.
