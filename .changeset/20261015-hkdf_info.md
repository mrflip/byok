**Every field a vault writes is now verified when it is opened**, but for the bundle's `unlock_epoch` and which envelopes its `unlocks` list holds (`notes/security.md` says why). A passkey's key is made with its vault and its envelope in the HKDF `info` (the scope, `vault_id`, the bundle's `registered_at`, the purpose, and the envelope's public fields), the same context its wrap is bound to. Every unlock envelope's wrap also binds the bundle's `registered_at`. A lockbox's encryptions bind its whole public header, the `note`, `registered_at` and `changed_at` included, so a backend that alters one makes `read()` refuse it as `corrupt_record` (the locked listing still shows them unverified). A remembered unlock binds its `ask_passphrase_at` under its own format tag, so a deadline moved on the device reads as no remembered unlock.

No compatibility, as before 1.0: every vault written before this cannot be opened. Its passphrase is refused as `wrong_passphrase` and its passkeys as `wrong_passkey`; its lockboxes read as `corrupt_record`; a remembered unlock is forgotten, with a warning through the logger, and the vault asks for the passphrase. Destroy such a vault (`destroy()` needs no unlock) and make it anew. Nothing changes shape: the stored records hold the fields they held.
